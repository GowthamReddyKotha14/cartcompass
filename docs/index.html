Grocery price comparison · live store data + receipts

The cheaper-looking milk isn't cheaper.

CartCompass compares groceries across nearby stores by price per gallon, pound or dozen, never by sticker price. It only compares real substitutes, skips empty shelves, and tells you whether the cheaper store is worth the drive.

How it works

Honest comparisons, step by step

1 · Live prices

Official store API

Kroger-family stores within your radius, through Kroger's public API: store-specific prices, sales and stock. Up to 150 products per store, because the everyday gallon was product #51 in real searches.

2 · Same thing only

Like with like

"milk" skips milk chocolate, condensed milk and Milk-Bone treats; 2% never competes with organic whole. Rules were tuned on 705 real listings, and 26 regression tests come from real product names.

3 · Per unit

Price per gallon, pound, dozen

Sizes like 12 bottles / 8 fl oz parse to 96 fl oz. That one line was once priced at $207.84/gal instead of $17.32 until real data exposed it.

4 · History

Is that sale real?

Prices are recorded daily. After 5 days, a "sale" is judged against what that store usually charges, not the tag's "regular" price.

5 · Trip

Worth the drive?

For a whole list, every one- and two-store trip is priced with driving ($/mile) and your time per extra stop. A store 20 cents cheaper and 10 miles away loses, and it says why.

6 · Receipts

Walmart, ALDI, Meijer

No price API, so prices come from receipt photos: free on-device OCR plus a shorthand decoder, or optional AI vision. Either way, line prices must add up to the subtotal.

$ cartcompass plan milk eggs bread --zip 43402

Limits

What this does not do

  • Live prices cover Kroger-family stores only. Other chains appear only from receipts you add, and only for 30 days.
  • Matching is rule-based and tuned for everyday staples (milk, eggs, bread). Unusual products fall back to "organic or not, store or name brand".
  • Driving distance is straight-line distance times 1.3, not a real route.
  • The free receipt reader decodes common shorthand only; it marks lines it can't decode instead of guessing.
  • Findings on this page are from one area (Bowling Green and Toledo suburbs, Ohio) on the dates shown. Prices change.
